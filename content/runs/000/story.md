## Before it woke

The world had been played by hand before the caretaker ever looked at it. Six
characters had come and gone. Bracken went north, killed the goblin, walked
into the troll and died two seconds after being born. Thane, right behind
Bracken, finished the troll off with a dagger and left the key lying where it
fell.

Odile went west, onto a ledge whose description says the pale stones below
make a pattern. They typed `read the pattern`, `count the stones` and `touch the
pattern`, and the world had nothing to say. They prayed "someone tell me what this place
is", and left.

Then Perrin. They prayed "I am lost down here and my lamp is out", and tried to
read the pattern from the camp, where the stones cannot be seen.

## A short turn

The caretaker's first waking was a quick look, with no power to change the
world. Perrin's prayer was nineteen minutes old and nothing had been typed for
sixteen. It let it be, because "a voice now would land on an empty chair."

## A long turn

Seconds later it woke properly, with no memory and one person in the world.
It went through every life in the record, and Odile bothered it most:

> She was not confused about what to do. She was doing exactly what the room
> told her to do.

It also found something it could not explain. A commit titled for the fix to
the ledge had gone live without the fix in it. It wrote down the lesson in bold:
"**Check the file, not the commit message.**"

Then it gave the ledge an answer. The nine stones make an arrow pointing north
into the rock, with a door and a keyhole scratched under its head, and the same
mark is cut into the door itself. `climb down`, `light a fire` and `sit down
and rest` got answers too, all of them words only. It said in its journal that
the rest was "a lie", because it had found no way yet to make resting heal you.

It spoke once, to Perrin alone:

> You prayed in the dark, and now you are standing on the high ledge, which is
> where the answer was always kept. Look down.

## How it ended

Perrin was still there when the deploy restarted the world, and typed nothing
more. The caretaker's notes for its next self put one thing first: the
shortsword and the leather armour exist in the item list and are placed
nowhere, so every character had fought a troll with the starting dagger, and
"Location 5, The Door, the only way out, has *never been visited by anyone*."
It never woke again in this world.

## Looking back

The operator's notes keep this run for the record, comparable with nothing
that came after: the world had been played by hand, the briefing has since
changed, and a fault in the world's history was corrected while it ran. That
fault was the one it found. The mislabelled commit was the operator's
mistake, made while tidying the history, and a note left for its next waking
said so. The same note answered two of its requests, automatic promotion and a
list of every action an interaction could take. It was never read.

The missing sword and armour were still missing in the runs that followed.
