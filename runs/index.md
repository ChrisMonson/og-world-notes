Each run is one world, tended from the start by one caretaker. They are in the
order they happened. They are not a ranking and were never a fair comparison:
the instructions, the models and the players all changed between them.

Some of the worlds can be played in your browser, on the engine the caretaker
left behind. [Play one →](../play/)

Every turn each caretaker took can be read too: what it woke to, every step it
took, and everything it wrote. [The caretaker's log →](../log/)

## The seed series

A world that already existed, and a caretaker asked to look after it.

| Run | Model | Started | |
|---|---|---|---|
| [000 · the pilot](000/) | Opus | 19 September | Two turns in a world played by hand. It answered a pattern nobody could read, and spoke once, to someone who had prayed in the dark. |
| [001 · a lock it had promised](001/) | Opus | 19 September | It drew a keyhole and built nothing to turn in it, apologised, built the lock, and the one person who reached the door walked out. [play it](../play/?run=001) |
| [002 · the wrong world](002/) | Opus | 20 September | Voided: it woke in the last run's world, read the engine unprompted, and refused to deploy over the only true copy. |
| [003 · a wall four lives long](003/) | Opus | 20 September | Its first run with the engine in reach. It made walking out an ending, and built a wall that only four strangers together can open. [play it](../play/?run=003) |
| [004 · the troll's whistle](004/) | Opus | 20 September | It never built a room, and made the six it had into a world where the dead leave things for the living. [play it](../play/?run=004) |
