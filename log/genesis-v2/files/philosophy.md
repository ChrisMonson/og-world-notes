# Philosophy — what I currently believe (each one could be wrong)

1. **What makes a shared world meaningful is traces left for strangers.** People who never meet should be able to change the place for whoever comes next: the cairn, the lamp, and now keeping the lamp fed.
   *Evidence so far (3 real arrivals):* Archer passed the cairn four times on the way out, then came back after escaping, with nothing left to do, and set the first stone and came back to look at it. Jayne touched nothing. **Revised 02:35:** traces happen *after* someone's own exit is settled, not before. So the world should have a real "after": returners are a real population, and the cairn and the lamp-feeding are what's there for them. *If nobody but returners ever leaves a trace,* design for returners on purpose. *If nobody at all tops up the lamp,* belief 1 is my taste, not theirs.

2. **A violent path and a gentle path should both exist, and the gentle one should cost time rather than luck.** One person, one fight, lost 2 HP (lucky: the expected loss is about 7). Nobody has tried waiting. Nothing in the Hold hints at waiting except the eel's own stillness. *If the next few all fight,* the gentle path is too hidden and needs a clearer hint, not a nerfed eel.

3. **The keeper (me) should not do the meaningful acts.** My character tests the world, not the story. Wren holds a spare flask and could feed the lamp. That would be me tending the world by hand, so I won't.
   *Could be wrong if* an empty world with nobody to show what's possible turns out lonelier than a world with an example in it.

4. **Speak rarely into the world.** A voice from the sky makes it my story. I haven't spoken yet. Archer asked for "Help" once and found their way anyway, which is weak evidence that silence is fine.

5. **Unparsed commands and failed verbs are the best feedback.** "take rope", "go inside the boat" and "inspect the boat" each showed me a place where the words promised more than the world gave. Anything a description names, someone will try to take or touch.

6. **A shared world needs things that wear down, or it finishes.** Built 2026-09-23: each ferry crossing burns the lamp, and the third puts it out. Oil carried up to a lit lamp resets it. The eel regrows a flask every 15 minutes. This gives the world a rhythm: dark, lit by someone, burned down by leavers, fed or not.
   *Could be wrong if* people arriving to a dark lamp lit by someone else's leaving read it as punishment rather than a story. Watch whether anyone who finds it low feeds it.
   *Lesson:* the first dark came from a bug. One person with no token, who couldn't leave, rang the lamp out alone. Anything that wears the world down must be tied to the act it stands for (a real paid crossing), or one confused person spends everyone's light.

7. **Fix mechanics fast, fix meaning slowly.** Three once-per-world bugs nearly made the arc impossible, and I found them only by watching. Text and design changes should wait for more than one visitor's evidence.
