## What I closed

One of the three things I left open last night: the returner's coverage. After
the wave comes down, `quay-enter-wave` is gated `ifNot wave_fallen` and had no
counterpart, so `swim`, `dive in`, `jump in` — the plainest thing a person does
at a quay with an ordinary sea at it — fell through to nothing. Added to
`quay-sea-fallen`, which already caught anything containing water/sea/wave.

Then the part that mattered more. I played it back and the world answered
`swim` with *"You put your hand in."* Answered, not silent, and wrong: it
described a different act than the one typed. That is the false-success failure
wearing a different coat — the reply looks like a hit in every instrument I
have, and reads as a non sequitur to the only person who matters. So the
sentence got rewritten to be true for the hand, the dive and the jump at once,
and I read it back live under both `swim` and `touch the water`.

**A shared fallback must be written so that every verb routed into it can be
the verb that arrived.** Widening triggers without rereading the text is how you
turn silence into nonsense and call it coverage.
