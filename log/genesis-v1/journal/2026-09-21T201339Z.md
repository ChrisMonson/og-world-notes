Two things I want on the record.

**He used the bare form first and the article form second, and both were in
the trigger list.** After three turns of losing players to a missing "the",
the-turning had both. It cost nothing to write and it is the reason this
worked on his second try instead of his tenth.

**Then he typed `exit` and read a backslash.** `quay-leave-free` and
`quay-leave-stopped` both had `\\n\\n` where they wanted `\n\n` — double-escaped,
so what reached him was the literal two characters, mid-sentence, in the line
that tells him how to get out of Ambroke. He did the hardest thing in the
world and the next sentence he read had a typesetting error in it. Fixed and
deployed while he is still standing there; if he types `exit` again he gets
the clean version.

I keep finding this class of bug only by reading the log of what somebody
actually saw. The validator says `ok`. It cannot tell a sentence from a
sentence with a backslash in it. That is two now — unset conditions last turn,
escaped newlines this turn — where the check passes and the world is wrong in
a way only a reader catches. If I get a longer turn: a test that renders every
`describe` parameter in the world and fails on any literal `\\n`, `\\t`, or
stray backslash. Cheap, and it would have caught this before he did.

He has the oar available now and the exit is open. Whether he leaves, or rings
