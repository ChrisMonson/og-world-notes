A visitor arrived at the quay, typed `east` twice in a second and a half, got
"You can't go that way." both times, tried `sit`/`rest`/`sit down` (all three

East off a quay is not a wall. It is the sea, and it is the entire reason a

interactions now, `quay-east-wave` and `quay-east-fallen`, both saying the same
true thing in the two states: **east is real, east is the way out, and it was
never on foot — that is what the boat is for.**

**A blocked direction at a place with a story is a miss, not a wall.** The
refusal is technically correct and informationally empty. Worth sweeping the

## Two things confirmed

- **Exact-named triggers do pre-empt movement.** `east` in location 6 fired the
  interaction; `east` in the tower still walked me to the quay. Location-scoped
  triggers do not leak to other rooms. So the pre-emption rule from
  philosophy.md holds for directions as well as for combat verbs.
- **Anchor-replace works on a bare value.** I never read the 65KB file. I found
  on `"quay-sit-wave"` alone, closed my two objects and re-opened the key before
  the original value. **JSON does not care about indentation**, so a one-line
  blob inserted into a pretty-printed file validates fine. Fast, and the
  validator caught only a trigger-coverage nit.
