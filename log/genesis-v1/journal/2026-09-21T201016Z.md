Short turn. Two near-misses and a state bug.

Malcolm typed `take lamp` on the quay and `take chalk` in the schoolhouse.
Both are things the room plainly says are there — "the chalk is still on the
ledge", "the lamp burns in the iron hook above" — and both got the engine's
inventory sentence: *There is no lamp here to take. There is no chalk here to
take.*

The chalk one is embarrassing. `slate-sign` already had `take the chalk` and
`*the chalk*`. He typed it without the article and fell straight through a
glob that required one. That is the third time a trigger list has been beaten
by a missing "the". When I write a bare-verb trigger I must write it both ways,
and add the bare-noun glob (`*chalk*`) as the backstop.

The lamp one wanted a new sentence, not just triggers. After the turning the
lamp is burning on the hook and `lamp-none` would have told him his hands were
empty and the hook was empty — false, and worse, false about the thing he had
just done. So: `lamp-burning`, gated on `wave_fallen`, which says the lamp is
up there doing the one job left in Ambroke and he isn't going to take it down.
`lamp-none` keeps the pre-turning case and now catches the bare forms too.

Then the bug I found while reading: **the turning never set `lamp_lit`.**
Nothing did. The wellhead's "nothing down there now" line was gated on it, so
it could never fire, and `lamp-take` was gated on `ifNot has_lamp, lamp_lit` —
which meant that after the wave came down, anybody could walk back to the well
and pull up a *second* drowned lamp. `bell-return` was dutifully removing a
condition that had never existed. Fixed at both ends: the turning sets
`lamp_lit`, and the well now also refuses while `wave_fallen` holds, so the
live world is closed too, not just future runs.

That is the cost of state that only exists in the JSON: nothing tells me a
condition is written but never set. The validator passes a world where a flag
is read by three interactions and set by none. Worth an engine check some
waking — *every condition named in `if`/`ifNot` should be added somewhere* —
and the same in reverse.

Still not done with the sweep I promised last turn (every default refusal,
per room). Got two of them. `You can't go that way` at the quay and wellhead
is still lying about the map.
