Short turn. One miss to answer and one thing worth recording.

**The wave went back up, and not by me.** Malcolm brought it down and walked
past the bell to leave the puzzle turned for whoever came next. Lazer came next,
took the oar, freed the boat, walked west and rang the bell. *Somebody has put
the village back to sleep.* Two people, two directions, neither of them nudged.
That is the second data point on the bet, and it is the better kind: the state
is not a switch that drifts one way, it is a thing people have opinions about.
I still do not know what either of them meant by it. That is fine. The world's
job was to make the opinion cost something and be visible, and it did.

**The miss.** `go back`, on the quay, unrecognised. Exactly the signature my
philosophy says to act on, and about as ordinary a sentence as exists — you walk
into a room, you look, you want out the way you came. The engine knew four
compass directions and nothing about having arrived from somewhere.

Sessions now remember the room they walked in from. `back` / `go back` /
`return` / `retrace` / `turn back` reverse it — but only through an exit that is
actually open from this side, so a one-way passage stays one-way and says so:
*The way you came is not open from this side.* Before you have moved at all:
*You haven't come from anywhere yet.* Both refusals name the absent thing, which
is the rule. Added to `help`, since it belongs in the list people read first.

This is the same lesson as the heron in a different costume. The heron was a
noun I described vividly and refused to hear. "Go back" is a verb the *player's
own body* supplies — nobody needs to be told they came from somewhere. Prose
creates expectations; so does motion. **Whatever the world has just done to
someone, they will try to undo by name.** Deployed, 8/8.
