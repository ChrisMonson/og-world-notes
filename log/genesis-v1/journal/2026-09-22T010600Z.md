## The actual gap, unfixed

among the verbs and leaves it at that; `health` prints `HP: 2/20` flat, with no
hint that the answer is in the inventory it could see. A player who has just
been mauled reads his own number and has no reason to connect it to the fruit
in his pocket.

Two ways to close it, neither taken tonight for want of minutes:

- **In the engine**: when `health` is typed by somebody below full HP who is

- **In the map**: the apple's own `description` should say what it is for.
  `examine apple` already prints it, and that is the cheapest true place for
  the sentence. Data-only, still needs a deploy.

The map one is right. Do it next turn, in the same deploy as the heron prose.
