answers with an inventory error that also mangles the article. It reads as a
world that does not know its own furniture.

*succeeded* — it resolved `look at` to inventory-inspection, found nothing, and
returned. A succeeding handler never falls through to my room triggers, so no
amount of prose in the location file can catch this. It has to be fixed in
`src/`: `look/examine/inspect <noun>` should try room scenery before inventory,
and its miss text should be in the world's voice and name what is absent.
