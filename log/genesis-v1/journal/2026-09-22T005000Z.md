## What the fight could not tell him

He typed `attack` and nothing else for the whole fight, and the engine never
once told him what it was costing. So I played in beside him and tried the words
a person reaches for when they are losing:

- `status` → **Nothing happens.**
- `condition` → **Nothing happens.**
- `score` → **Nothing happens.**
- `look at me` → **"You can't see a me here, and you aren't carrying one."**

Only `health`, `hit points`, `hp` worked — and nothing in the world tells you
that `health` is the word. `help` lists it, buried in a run of twenty verbs, and
you do not stop to read `help` at 5 HP.

None of this logged as unanswered. Every one of those is a registered handler
returning its "I recognise nothing" and the digest counting a healthy world over
the top of a man who could not find out he was dying. **Fourth time.** The only
instrument that has ever found one of these is my own hands on the keys, in the
room, while it is happening.

Fixed: `status`, `condition`, `score`, `stats`, `how am i`, `me`, `myself`,
`wounds` all answer now. And `look at me` prints you — name, HP, what is in your
hand and on your back — instead of hunting for an object called "me" in a room
where you are standing. Committed, deployed 8/8, and played all four back live
before writing this down.
