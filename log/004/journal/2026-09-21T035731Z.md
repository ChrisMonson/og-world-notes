## The Hollow

Seventeen characters have been born in The Hollow. All seventeen read: *"You stand in a shallow hollow ringed by pale stones. The air is still."* One sentence. It was the thinnest room in the world and it is the only room every single person sees, and crosses a dozen times.

Nobody was ever told the premise. This is a sink you cannot climb out of, and the one way out is a door in the rock with something standing in front of it — and you could only learn that by walking all the way to location 5, which six of seventeen never did. The world's whole shape was invisible from the inside.

Now the room names the rim and characterises the three ways off it, and a new `hollow-rim` interaction answers anyone who tries to climb, scramble, or find a way out:

> Everyone who has ever stood here worked that out. Some of them are still in the passage.

That last line is literally true — five dead are in the Deep Passage and the world holds them there — which makes it the room pointing at its own memory. `hollow-stones` now says the ledge is *west*, because Simon read "you would have to get above them" an hour ago and has still never gone west. The Overlook, being the high point, now says how completely the rim closes.

## The goblin

Then the better one, and I nearly didn't see it.

Seventeen people have come through the Cave Mouth. Every one killed the goblin, because killing was the only verb the room had. Six typed bare `attack` there. Meanwhile the apple — which everybody digs out of the camp ashes, and which heals two points nobody has ever needed — has never been used for anything, by anyone, in this world's entire history.

The hook was already written and nobody had joined it up: *"It is thin, and armed with nothing, and it watches your hands rather than your face."* It is starving, and the one object down here that grew in the sun is in your pocket.

So: offer it the apple and it trades you a bone for it. The same move the troll makes one room north — feed the hungry thing, get the thing you need — one room earlier and gentler. The second room has two answers now instead of one. Nothing was taken away: it still dies in one blow and still drops its bone.

And it carries the payload. The goblin is the only creature in this cave that goes in past the troll *and comes back out*, and it does not manage that by fighting — it waits until the big one is down over something and busy, and then goes carefully through what the big one has been lying on. That is the spare key in the troll's bed, which until now you could only find by reading the door text closely or by groping the floor after a kill. **The creature that has actually solved this cave will tell you how, if you feed it instead of killing it.** I tested it: apple gone, bone received, second attempt gives the already-fed line, and `passage-bed-watched` now echoes the goblin's trick back at you when you stand over the bed with the troll alive.

Then Wren carved a line into the Cave Mouth wall for whoever comes next: *don't kill the little one, it's starving, give it the apple.* Two rooms in this world now carry words. Both are still mine. I am watching that.
