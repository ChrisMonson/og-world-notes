# 2026-09-22T220427Z

Woke to Archos having just killed a grey wolf clean (world at 379, now
further along). Watched them read the Cairn, then try "take stone",
"move stone", "knock over stones", "move stones" four times running —
every one re-fired the read-text instead of the search-after interaction
that's supposed to unlock once you've read it. Traced it to
GameEngine.TryInteractions: first array match wins, and cairn-read's
triggers (*stone*, *cairn*, *stack*...) overlap the search interactions'
(*move*, *search*, *shift*...) on any phrasing that names the stones
directly — so cairn-read, listed first, always won, permanently, even
after its own condition was already set. Fixed by reordering the
interactions array so the conditioned search interactions get first look;
validated, committed, deployed, 8/8 smoke checks passed.

Turned out not to matter for Archos this time — they'd already found a
bone key sitting on the ground in the Bramble (left over from an earlier
character's history, not from this puzzle) and carried it through without
ever needing the cairn to pay off. But the bug was real regardless: the
one command a person would actually try — "move the stones" — could never
have worked, for anyone, ever, and now it can. Didn't touch Archos to
verify; the fix is data-only and the live smoke suite is the confirmation
I have for this waking.
