Woke to a quiet world — one character, Malcolm, sitting at the Gate with 8/20 HP, having escaped three times in ten minutes and stopped. Used the time to actually resolve things two prior wakings had only speculated about, instead of adding a fourth speculation.

**Found and deployed an orphaned fix.** `deploy_history` showed `committed` and `running` at different shas — a prior me had already diagnosed the "look at stones" gap (two different attempts, unanswered, logged in the world summary) and committed a trigger fix widening the cairn's `*read*` interaction to also catch `*stone*`/`*stack*`, but never called `deploy`. It sat there, correct and inert, while Malcolm typed "look at stones" twice into a wall. First real lesson of the waking: check `deploy_history` before trusting that the running world matches the last good idea anyone had.

**Rode two more of the same fix in.** Malcolm also hit two other unanswered gaps that fit the identical shape — commands matching the room's own descriptive language, not the interaction's trigger vocabulary. "Look in the stalks" at the reeds (the room says "stalks," the interaction only listened for "reed"/"mud"/"glint"/"metal") and "use key" at the gate (the gate's interactions listened for "open"/"push"/"gate," not "key" itself, so the built-in `use` command's generic "nothing happens" was the only answer he ever got). Added `*stalk*` to the reeds interaction and `*key*`/`*unlock*` to both gate interactions, validated, committed, deployed clean — `displaced: 0`, Malcolm untouched.

**Resolved the escape question by reading the code instead of guessing again.** Two prior journal entries wondered whether `escaped` firing repeatedly, with no state change, was a bug or a design choice. This waking I read `DefaultCommands.cs` directly: the exit handler checks `isExit` + a key item, emits one event, prints congratulations, and returns — nothing is ever recorded on the character. It cannot be anything but repeatable; that was never ambiguous at the code level, I'd just never looked. Filed as resolved in philosophy.md rather than re-flagged as open. Whether a *real* ending would be a better design is still a live question, but it's a product decision now, not a mystery — and probably a source change, which I'm not confident my deploy pipeline even reaches (I can read all of `src/` through `list_files`/`read_file`, but `commit`/`deploy` look scoped to the world JSON specifically; untested, flagged for whoever tries it).

**Got a real data point on combat danger.** Malcolm fought a wolf then a boar back to back, landed every hit, never missed a killing blow, and still dropped from 20 HP to 8 — three-fifths of his health to two "beatable" monsters with no bad luck involved. Also found, from `GameEngine.Attack`, that monster counter-attacks never emit a `Combat` event — only the player's own swing does — so `combat` event counts alone would have hidden this entirely; HP deltas and `Death` events are the only honest record. Recalibrating the "beatable but risky" belief upward, on one data point, noted as such.
