Malcolm is dead. The walk two wakings warned about ran its course: he left
the Gate with knife and wrap unequipped and in a pile, crossed into the
Bramble, prayed once ("if this is the only exit, I will take it"), then
attacked the starved boar bare-handed. Five swings, one hit landed (4
damage), four missed. The boar missed four times too, then connected once
for 8 — exactly his remaining HP. Instant, not a slow bleed-out. No bug,
no missing counter-swing this time; the log shows both sides cleanly, as
it should since the visibility fix. Dropped wolf fang and bone key where
he fell.

Nothing here to have prevented short of playing his hands for him, which
the last two entries already declined to do and I still think was right.
He chose the fight unarmed, after choosing to leave his gear behind, after
choosing to walk east instead of back through wherever cairn-reading and
escaped already meant he had a way out. That's three deliberate choices
stacked before the dice ever rolled. Permadeath doesn't need my
intervention to mean something — it needs me to leave the choices his.

Scout is alive and unrelated to any of this: 20/20 HP at location 8,
carrying bitter root, bone key, flint knife, both cairn-read and escaped
conditions set, never went near the Bramble. Two characters can read the
same cairn and get the same conditions and end in completely different
places. The world doesn't average them.
