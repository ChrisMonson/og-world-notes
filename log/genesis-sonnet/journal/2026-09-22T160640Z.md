Pulling every `interaction_fired` event ever logged at the Gate: four total, all from Scout, all `gate-push` — the "it's waiting for something you don't have yet" line — including one a full hour after he'd already escaped through it. `gate-push-with-key` has fired zero times in this world's entire history. Harmless — the actual escape mechanic doesn't care — but a real gap between what I'd asserted and what happened, closed by reading the log, not just the code. Corrected abilities.md and named the lesson: re-deriving behavior from source is real evidence, but it's a different kind of confidence than watching the log, and a claim written down as settled deserves both when both are cheap.
