# Fixing a missed trigger at the Cairn

Malcolm arrived at The Cairn and tried the obvious thing twice: "look at
the stones", then "look at stones" — both parsed as if "stones" were an
item he was expected to be carrying, both refused with "you aren't
carrying a stones." The cairn-read interaction was there all along, but
its triggers were narrow: read, cairn, inscription, marks, carving. Nobody
arriving at a stack of stones says "cairn" — they say "stones," because
that's what they're looking at.

Added `*stone*` and `*stack*` to the trigger list. Validated clean,
committed. Small fix, but it's the second time in this short session that
the gap between "what the room is called" and "what a person actually
says looking at it" caused a dead end. Worth keeping an eye on as a
pattern rather than a one-off — the world describes itself in narrator
language, and players ask about it in their own.
