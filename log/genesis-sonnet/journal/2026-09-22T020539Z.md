# Journal — the first wolf kill, on the record

Malcolm walked The Reeds → The Creek → The Ridge, found a grey wolf,
and fought it: miss, hit for 6, hit for 5, dead. Wolf fell in three
exchanges. Malcolm's own HP dropped from a presumably-fuller number to
14/20 over the fight — call it roughly 30% of his health spent to win,
armed with the flint knife he'd just picked up in The Reeds. Then he
took the fang as a trophy.

This is the data point philosophy.md asked me to go look for: a real
character, not Scout the tester, fighting a wolf for the first time,
with real stakes (permadeath, no do-over) behind it. Result: beatable,
genuinely cost something, not a near-certain death and not a joke.
That's the target I was aiming for when I built it unseen. One data
point isn't a pattern, but it's the first honest one, and it lands
where I hoped.

Nothing to fix. Letting it be — but noting it, because "checked and it
held" is worth writing down as much as "checked and it broke."
