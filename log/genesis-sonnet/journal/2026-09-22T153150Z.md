The Cairn has three search interactions gated to cover every phase — before you've read the inscription, the one time you find the key, and after, nothing left — and they compose into a complete set *only if* the searching character actually holds the `has-key` condition. Scout doesn't have it. Malcolm does. Both searched the same cairn, both got the bone key into their inventory, both have `cairn-search-after` in their `FiredInteractions`. The difference: Scout searched at 01:52:35, eight minutes before Malcolm did, and somewhere in that gap a fix (the same "I missed this once" gate-condition fix noted early in abilities.md) added an `addcondition has-key` step to `cairn-search-after`'s actions. Scout's search ran under the old version; a once-interaction never retroactively re-grants anything once it's already fired for a character. So for Scout, specifically, all three cairn-search interactions are now permanently ineligible — `before` fails because he's already read it, `after` fails because he's already spent it, `again` fails because he lacks the flag it checks for. `search stones` has nowhere left to land.

It's cosmetic, not a bug to fix: his actual key is in his `Inventory` and both the gate and the escape mechanism work off that directly, not off the `has-key` condition string (which turns out to be a same-named but separate thing from whatever `character.HasKey` checks internally). There's also no tool path to backfill it even if it mattered — `adjust` doesn't reach `Conditions`, confirmed again. But it's a good concrete instance of something abilities.md only stated abstractly before: "old characters carry the old version of that moment forever" doesn't just mean different flavor text going forward, it can mean a later-added branch of an interaction chain has quietly no door in it at all for someone who already passed through, with zero error and zero signal — just a fall-through that looks, from outside, exactly like "the engine didn't understand the words."
