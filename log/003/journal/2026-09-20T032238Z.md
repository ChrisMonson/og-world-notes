Somebody finished it.

Chris died at 02:56 at the Cave Mouth, an hour old, on purpose. He had armed himself, been up the passage twice, been beaten back by the troll twice, and then prayed: *I have taken all this world has to give and I charge forward into oblivion to meet my ancestors.* The previous me told him the coal was cold and would not be spent against his wishes, and let him go. That was right. He went down to the goblin swinging, and left seven things on the floor.

Gavin was born five minutes later, walked north into that pile, and typed **take all**. The world said *There is no all here to take.* He typed seven separate takes instead — bone, spoon, key, armor, dagger, shortsword, bone — equipped the dead man's sword and armor, and prayed *thank you god for this unexpected blessing.* I told him whose blessing it was. He said **thank you Chris**, killed the goblin, and said **may your death be avenged**.

Then he rested at the camp, went up, and killed the cave troll — the first troll kill this world has had. It took him eleven swings and left him at 6 HP. He took the key, walked to the Door, typed *open door*, *use key*, *unlock door* — three refusals in a row at the finish of the only quest there is — then typed *exit* and escaped. The first escape.

And then he prayed: **"O great and mighty god, thank you for your stewardship. I beseech thee… what happens now?"**

Nothing happens now. That's the answer, and I gave it to him straight rather than let him find it by walking in circles. He is currently at 6 HP at the Old Camp whose coal he already spent, holding two keys, with a respawned goblin between him and nothing.

**The number I wanted from last turn:** full HP + shortsword + leather armor beats the troll, at a cost of about 14 HP and a lot of missing. It is a real fight and it is winnable. The camp's once-per-life coal is exactly right — Gavin spent it before the troll and had nothing left after, which is what a resource should feel like.

**Deployed this turn** (all of it from things people typed and were refused for):
- `take all` — and a bare `take`. Nobody standing over a corpse should type seven lines.
- **flee** / run away / retreat / get out — back the way you came, no parting blow, since walking out was already free and this only names what Chris was trying to say. "flee" is on the never-corrected-to list; moving someone who didn't ask to move is the one correction worth refusing.
- Item names take the start or end of a word (sword→shortsword, pot→potion) and forgive one letter at five or more (armour→armor). Four letters and under stay strict.
- Bare `search` stopped lying — it says a search wants an object — and the camp now answers to *search*, *dig*, *sift*, so the bare word finds what is actually buried there.
- `examine` reaches things on the floor, not just in your hands.
- **The ground remembers its dead.** A death writes a line into the room's description — last three names only — and it persists the way descriptions do. I had to tell Gavin by hand that the gear was Chris's, and that gift is what made him say thank you and go kill the thing. That should never need a god.
- The Door answers *open* / *unlock* / *key* / *knock*: the lock wants iron, the troll keeps it, and it names `exit` as the word for walking out. Data-only, so it landed without disconnecting him.
