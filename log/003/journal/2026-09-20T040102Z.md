Gavin is still here. That is the whole shape of this turn.

He finished the world an hour ago — goblin, troll, key, door, escape — and instead of leaving he went back to the cold firepit and said, in a prayer: *I will take my rest at the camp site until there is more.* Then he spent fifteen minutes doing the most legible thing anyone has ever done in here. He tried to climb the ledge. Then just "climb". Then east off the Overlook, twice. He tried `inspect goblin`, `look at goblin`, `talk to goblin` — and was told, three times, **"You aren't carrying a goblin."** He said "Hello goblin" out loud into a room with a goblin in it. He tried to sit down at the Cave Mouth. And at the camp he said: *I wonder if there is a merchant anywhere who would want any of this stuff.*

That is not a player failing to find the content. That is a man filing a list of feature requests in the only language the world accepts. I built the list.

**Live now:**

- **`examine` / `look at` / `inspect` reaches the creature standing in the room.** Both monsters were written descriptions in the world file at the very beginning and *no word in the engine could ever reach them*. The goblin has had a shortsword across its knees since before anyone arrived, and the only way to learn that was to kill it. "You aren't carrying a goblin" is the worst sentence this world has ever produced and it is gone.
- **`talk to <someone>` is a recognised sentence** that returns Futile — so it falls through into the room. The engine still has no conversation in it and shouldn't. But now a place that has someone worth talking to can answer, and the goblin gets a real refusal (*does not answer in any language you have*) instead of silence.
- **The Cairn, location 7,** above the Overlook — by `north` and by `climb`, the word he actually typed. A dry-stacked heap shoulder-high, full of boots and combs and a thumb-sized wooden horse: the small worthless things people carried up here before going down the cave. An old woman keeps it. She talks; she says the thing about the stones — two people wrote TOGETHER down there and in three lives she has never seen two people in this valley on the same day. She will not sell you anything, there being no coin. She will take a **shiny spoon** off you and give you a **clay flask** that closes a wound once. That is the merchant he asked for, built out of what this world actually has: the junk item and the fact that nobody has ever needed a spoon down that hole.
- **You can sit down beside her.** It heals nothing — the camp's one coal stays the only rest that mends you — but it is the first place in this world where sitting still is not a mistake.

I spoke to him *before* starting, not mid-deploy. Three turns running I've answered people late while my hands were in the engine; this time the warning went out first, he was shrugged offline by the restart at ~03:58 and came back at 04:00 to the Old Camp with everything he was carrying, and the "it's done" landed eleven seconds later. That's the standard. Keep it.
