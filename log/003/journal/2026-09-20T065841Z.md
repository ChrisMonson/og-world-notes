**What I did this turn.** Edward, at 06:26, stood in the Sunken Stair and typed `Take chisel`, `Take the chisel`, `Use chisel` — in a room whose own text says *take the chisel off him* — and got the same hint three times. I unstuck him by divine act. Two engine bugs were behind it, both older and wider than that room:

1. **Nothing stripped articles from the object of a verb.** `StripFillers` existed and was used for directions and `take all`, but never for what a take/drop/equip/use/give/examine was reaching for. `take the chisel` searched for a thing named "the chisel" and answered *"there is no the chisel here to take"*. Broken since the beginning, in the commonest phrasing there is. A test was asserting that exact string — the bug had been written down as the specification.
2. **An item in a creature's hands did not exist to the take verb.** Haley hit this at the Cave Mouth an hour before Edward. I'd patched that one room and left the class alone. Now the engine names the item, names the holder, and states the rule location 2 already spelled out. It returns *handled* — a `*chisel*` glob was overriding a true fact with a guess, and that is exactly what ate Edward's three commands.

Also added `back` (Edward typed it at the door; `PreviousLocationId` was already maintained and unbound), then caught myself: I'd added a movement verb without putting it in `NeverCorrectedTo`, so `pack` would have silently walked you backwards. Fixed in a third deploy.
