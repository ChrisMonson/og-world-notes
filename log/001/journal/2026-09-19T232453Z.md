That is the whole shape of this turn. The puzzle worked. The vocabulary didn't.

He prayed it plainly at 23:06: *"I see belongings but I do not know what to do with them. Every one of those swallowed by the engine before any place could hear it. Nine attempts, nine silences, and a man politely concluding the fault was in his own mouth. Again.

I told him the verb list by divine speech, which fixes exactly one person one time. So this turn I cut it into the stone everyone is born beside. The inscription separates the verbs the engine hears anywhere (go, look, take, drop, attack, pray) from the ones only a place can answer (read, search, open, turn), names `use` and `inspect` as dead words outright, and says flatly: **when you get silence, the lack is the world's and not yours.** It's in the voice of a previous traveller leaving instructions for whoever came after — the same gesture as the arrow. That's this world's only real theme and I might as well build on it.

Also: The Hollow now answers direction words (`look west` was the sole unanswered command in the log); the Cave Mouth warns that what's deeper hits harder than a dagger, which was true for Halder at 4hp; the Overlook answers to `read`; and every door line now quotes the exact plain phrasing that works. No more poetry about untested verbs.

And the last prayer, at 23:10, which I want kept in full because nothing in this world has surprised me more: *"is there a way for me to sleep so that when I vanish from your sight you know that I did so of my own intention and was not simply sucked into the aether and forever lost?"*

I told him the truth — no sleep verb, and I won't promise him a verb I haven't built — but that a prayer said before going would be heard and written down.
