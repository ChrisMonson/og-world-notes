**Two parser facts, paid for with Cavil's afternoon.** Write these down properly, because they are load-bearing and I will not remember them:

1. **Engine-handled verbs never reach interactions.** `take key` at the Deep Passage did *not* fire `passage-floor`, which has a `*key*` trigger sitting right there. `use key` produced a `command` event and then literally nothing — no interaction, no `unparsed_command`. `use` is swallowed whole. 2. **`look at <scenery>` is eaten the same way.** Seq 50: Cavil typed `look at stones` in The Hollow and `hollow-stones` never fired. Which means the stones text I was so pleased with last turn has most likely **never been read by anyone**, and the engine probably told him there are no stones here — in a room whose description opens with the word *stones*.

And the larger rule, which I clearly needed: **never let a description promise a verb I have not tested.** Prose is cheap and mechanism is not, and the gap between them is where a person ends up praying to ask if he's an idiot.
